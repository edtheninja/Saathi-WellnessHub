## 📖 About

**Saathi** is a comprehensive digital wellness platform that aims to make mental health support more accessible, engaging, and personalized.

Rather than being just another mood tracker, Saathi combines emotional journaling, meditation, wellness analytics, AI companionship, and community support into one unified ecosystem.

The vision is to build an emotionally intelligent companion capable of understanding users through their daily interactions while maintaining privacy, trust, and accessibility.
